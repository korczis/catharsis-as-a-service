Every question here has its own page, its own address and its own sources, so a single answer can be read, linked or corrected on its own. The short answer at the top of each page is the whole answer for a reader who stops there; everything below it is the same answer with its evidence attached.

Answers state what was found, in what kind of study, and what it does not settle. Where the research disagrees with itself, the question says so rather than choosing the tidier side. The evidence behind each answer is graded in the [evidence ledger](@/evidence/index.md), and the terms are defined in the [glossary](@/glossary/index.md).

This is general education, not a treatment plan, and no answer here can assess an individual situation.
