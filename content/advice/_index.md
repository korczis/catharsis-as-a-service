This library translates the research notes into practice. Each entry states one recommendation, the strength of the evidence behind it, concrete steps, and the conditions under which it does not apply.

Evidence is graded on a simple scale:

- **Meta-analytic:** supported by a quantitative synthesis of many studies.
- **Replicated experimental:** supported by controlled experiments from more than one research group.
- **Experimental:** supported by controlled experiments, with limited independent replication.
- **Observational:** supported by consistent observational and longitudinal studies, without experiments that establish cause.
- **Theoretical:** grounded in an established clinical or theoretical framework rather than in a single decisive experiment.

The entries are general education, not a treatment plan. People differ, and a recommendation that helps most participants in a study can be wrong for a specific person at a specific time.
