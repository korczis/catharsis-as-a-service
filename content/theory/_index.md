These essays explain the theories that the research notes rely on: where each theory came from, what it claims, the reasoning behind it, and which evidence supports or challenges it. Each one separates three kinds of statement: what a theory proposes, what studies designed to test it found, and what is interpretation by the author, in particular about the difference between relief and resolution. Contested positions are marked as contested, and every essay lists its references with DOIs where they exist.

The essays are written for an educated general reader. A theory is a way of organising evidence, not a description of any one person's experience, and nothing here replaces clinical assessment or treatment.
