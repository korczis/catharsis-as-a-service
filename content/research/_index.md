These notes summarise classical sources and peer-reviewed research relevant to the artifact. Each one separates three kinds of statement: what the cited studies found, where the evidence is mixed or limited, and what is interpretation by the author. Every note lists its references with DOIs where they exist, so each claim can be checked against its source.

The notes are written for an educated general reader. They are not systematic reviews, and they do not replace clinical assessment or treatment.
